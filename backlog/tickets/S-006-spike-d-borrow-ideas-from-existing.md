## Notes

- Recommendation: keep "build" (OD-7 default). Neither project has open registration, per-component honesty labels with evidence ids, or the descriptors/contract; loadsketch needs twelve steps across about ten files to add a component, which is the problem ADR 0003 #1 solves.
- Breakscale ported fdlibm because, per its header, `Math.log` differs on arm64 and in Safari; that is consistent with spike A (a different mechanism, a V8-version difference) on the point that matters. Its incident captions by simulated time match our `case.timeline`; both carry the request key downstream (ADR 0003 #3).
- Review (model-reviewer, pass with minor fixes; I re-checked the three most important claims against the source). Fixed: loadsketch has no portable math and its golden hashes are over raw doubles; my token-bucket example was wrong (Breakscale builds one on an existing hook with timestamp refill); Breakscale is separable and headless, not a single file; 22 of loadsketch's 25 checks are against formulas, one seed each, the deviation is about +3100%; Breakscale has no queueing-delay oracle but does check Zipf shares and loss rates; the gamma note now covers `pow` and the log domain; wording on what check-agents-md does, `cpuMs`, per-component honesty; added borrowed ideas (timestamp-based refill and frame-independent logs, design floors, metrics with no meaning show nothing), a component-set comparison, and what not to borrow (silent fallback for unknown kinds).
- New to-dos from this spike (listed in the note, not made into tickets yet): arm64 and Safari determinism run (V9, S-091); polar method for gamma service in S-022; `docs/accuracy.md` generated by S-040; schedule caps and seed validation (S-010b, S-050); catalog-generated agent guide (S-010b).
